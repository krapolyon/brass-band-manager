The files of FluidR3_GM/ are recorded samples of instruments, one file per
instrument, used by the MIDI player of the app.

They come from the Fluid R3 General MIDI SoundFont (FluidR3_GM.sf2) by Frank
Wen, released under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

They were rendered to one mp3 per note by:
- MIDI.js Soundfonts, Benjamin Gleitzman
  (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), for the instruments;
- its fork by Paul Rosen (https://github.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts),
  for percussion-mp3.js, the General MIDI drum kit.

The files are unchanged.
